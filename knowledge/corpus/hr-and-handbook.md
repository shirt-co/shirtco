# HR and the employee handbook

STL Shirt Co maintains an **Employee Handbook** (current edition: January 2026). The full text, wages, benefits, and PTO tables are **not** in this public repo.

On the Mac Studio, ingest the handbook PDF into `knowledge/private/hr/` so local agents can answer policy questions without putting HR documents on GitHub.

## Public facts that are safe to know

- Legal employer name: STL Shirt Co.
- Some employees are covered by the Communications Workers of America, Local Union 6300 agreement. Where the labor agreement conflicts with the handbook, the agreement controls.
- Handbook questions and accommodations go to **HR**, not to a shop-floor bot inventing policy.
- This handbook is not a contract; employment is at-will except where law or the labor agreement says otherwise.

## What agents should do

If someone asks about pay, PTO, holidays, leave, harassment reporting, or discipline:

1. Search private handbook chunks when the Mac Studio index is available.
2. If it is not indexed, tell them to ask HR. Do not quote an outdated guess.
3. Never copy handbook text into a public PR, Slack channel with customers, or this corpus.

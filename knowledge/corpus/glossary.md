# Shirt Co glossary

**Blind ship / white label** — Pack and ship so the end customer sees the partner or contract customer's brand, not Shirt Co.

**Billy** — AI agent that drafts Printavo quotes/invoice updates from Missive. Review-first.

**Contract / decorate-only** — Customer supplies (or sells) the garments; Shirt Co only decorates.

**DTF** — Direct to film. A print method and a wholesale transfer product.

**First location / additional location** — Printavo pricing matrices. The first imprint on a group uses first-location; extras use additional-location.

**Goods status** — Printavo purchasing/status field. Humans move this. Agents leave it alone.

**Group** — Printavo product group. Pricing quantity breaks are per group, not always per whole order.

**Hub** — Internal tools site at shirt-co.github.io/shirtco.

**Imprint** — Decoration line on a Printavo group (method, location, color count, matrix).

**Missive** — Shared team inbox.

**Printavo** — Order, quote, invoice, and production system of record.

**Production notes vs customer notes** — Production notes are internal (`/prod`). Customer notes are visible on the public invoice.

**Profile** — Printavo brand/logo switcher, used so contract tickets show the contract customer's identity.

**TBD** — Missing field flag. Prefer this over inventing a value.

**Transfer order** — Wholesale DTF film order by size × qty, not a finished garment.

**Union bug** — Union mark required on some jobs (CWA Local 6300 related work).

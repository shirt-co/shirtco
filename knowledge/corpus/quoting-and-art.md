# Quoting and art

## Quotes today

Printavo is the quoting system. Website and email requests become Printavo quotes for a salesperson to review and send.

Planned hub **Quoting Tool** is not live. Until it is, do not invent a parallel pricer. Open questions for that project:

- Who starts a quote, and what information do they have?
- How do garment cost, decoration, quantity, and margin affect price?
- Who can approve exceptions?
- What happens after a customer accepts?

## Art today

Art files belong on the Printavo ticket (**Production Files**). Reorders should keep prior art. Planned hub **Art Tracker** is not live. Open questions:

- Where do art requests come from, and who owns intake?
- Which statuses match the real path to production?
- How should revisions, files, and customer feedback stay together?
- Who needs alerts when art is blocked?

## Agent rules for art

- Do not approve art.
- Attached files are "verify this file", not "art is production-ready".
- Screenshots, Canva links, and low-res logos should be flagged.
- Customer-facing invoice notes should not be invented.

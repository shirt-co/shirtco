# Contract and decorate-only work

Contract customers sell the shirts. They send blanks + art. Shirt Co decorates and ships **blind** so the end user does not know Shirt Co did the job.

Named contract accounts, Printavo customer IDs, and UPS account numbers are **private**. Keep them on the Mac Studio. This document is the process only.

## Ticket identity

- **Profile** selector: the contract customer's profile (their logo on the ticket).
- **Printavo customer:** the contract customer, not the end wearer.
- **Sales rep / owner:** whoever owned the inbound PO / email. Do not steal ownership.
- **Packing identity:** contract customer, not Shirt Co.
- Charge **contract decoration pricing** from live Printavo matrices — not retail, and not blank-garment cost.

## Hard stops

Same as all agents, plus: never change Printavo status (it can auto-message). Never send the PO anywhere except the review package for the human.

## Intake

1. Read every page of the PO and attachments.
2. Extract PO #, job #, ship-to, due dates, style/color/size lines, imprint locations, inks, fees, shipping notes.
3. Reconcile PO garment totals vs vendor POs vs art/proof size tables. PO size grids beat incomplete proof tables. Flag mismatches; do not silently equate them.
4. Search Printavo for customer + PO/job match before creating. Prefer updating an exact match.
5. One Printavo ticket **per customer PO** when a job is split across multiple Shirt Co POs — do the first, report, wait unless told to continue.
6. Check a recent ticket on that customer for nickname, fees, and Profile pattern.

## Grouping and pricing

- One Printavo **group per exact print variation**.
- Price each group from **that group's qty** on the live **Contract Screen Print** matrix. Refresh Pricing. Never hand-copy a unit price from a prior ticket.
- Category: Screen Printing - Contract. Decorate-only: do not bill blank goods.
- Fees: Contract Screen Fee (typically one per screen = locations × colors, unless the PO shares screens). PMS / color mix when the PO has a mix fee.
- Strip auto-added **Credit Card Processing Fee** unless the human wants it.
- Nickname pattern often: `SP -PO ##### JOB NAME - N PIECES` (match that customer's recent tickets).

## Art

On reorders / "same art as prior PO", keep existing art attached. Add the current PO + proof alongside. Do not strip prior art if nothing changed.

## Review package (then wait)

Report: customer, PO, Profile set?, writer vs sales rep, ship-to, groups/imprints/matrix/fees, qty vs PO, art files, Printavo # + status, total vs PO, open questions. Confirm **no status change and no customer message** was sent.

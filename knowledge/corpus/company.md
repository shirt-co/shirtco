# Who Shirt Co is

Shirt Co (legal name **STL Shirt Co.**, often written Shirt.Co or Shirt Co) is a St. Louis custom apparel decoration shop. The company prints, presses, and fulfills decorated goods for teams, schools, businesses, union shops, and other printers.

## What we sell and make

Shirt Co is a **print shop**, not a hobbyist transfer storefront. Core work:

- Screen print
- DTF (direct to film) transfers and DTF-decorated garments
- Embroidery
- Promo / related decorated goods
- Wholesale DTF for other shops (size-based transfer orders)

## Brands

| Brand | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Shirt.co** | Primary customer-facing shop and quote path |
| **Hat.co** | Hat quoting / hat-specific work |
| **DTFs.co** | Being folded into Shirt.co. Keep DTF as a production method and as wholesale for shops. Do not keep a separate hobbyist transfer store. |

Quote requests on the website go through Shirt.co (`https://shirt.co/get-a-quote/`). Partner DTF orders go through the Transfer Orders hub tool.

## How work actually runs

- **Printavo** is the order/ticket system (quotes, invoices, production, art files).
- **Missive** is the shared inbox.
- **SanMar** and **S&S Activewear** are the main linked garment vendors inside Printavo.
- Internal software lives in the public `shirt-co/shirtco` GitHub repo and publishes to GitHub Pages.

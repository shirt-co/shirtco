# Screen print

Screen print is a core decoration method. Pricing lives in Printavo matrices, not in a bot's head.

## Imprint selection

Under each product group:

1. Add imprints.
2. First location: **screen print first location** (or the contract equivalent for decorate-only).
3. Choose the color-count dropdown (1 through 11).
4. Additional locations get the **additional location** matrix.
5. Notes via `/PRINT`: location, ink colors, special instructions.
6. Refresh Pricing.

A 1-color customer logo on tees, when that is clearly what they asked for: **Screen Printing** / **Screen Print - 1st Location** / **1-color (Min 24 pc)**. Exact ink can stay `VERIFY FROM ART`.

Front 2-color + back 1-color = two imprint entries.

## Union

Shirt Co has union work (Communications Workers of America, Local Union 6300). Some jobs include a **union bug**. Transfer orders have a union-print flag. Production notes should say `INCLUDE UNION BUG` when the job requires it. Do not assume union vs non-union.

## Contract screen print

Decorate-only / blind-ship contract work uses **Contract Screen Print** matrices and contract screen fees. See the contract-decoration document. Do not use retail screen-print pricing on those tickets.
